PL-300 Exam Prep

Practice for the Microsoft Power BI Data Analyst exam: 140 questions in the real exam formats with an explanation behind every answer, timed simulations, a study guide for every skill on the outline, weak-area tracking and a final review sheet.

It covers 140 original questions across the four PL-300 domains in single-answer, multiple-answer, drop-down, sequence, yes/no series and case study formats, with study notes on Power Query, data modeling, DAX, report design, workspaces and row-level security. It is written for candidates for the Microsoft Power BI Data Analyst Associate certification who want to practice, find their weak areas and do a last review.

Every branch states the trade-off that decided it, so the recommendation you end on comes with the reasoning attached — something you can paste into a design note or defend in a review.
