STORE DELIBERATELY

Store once, use many times

Avoid uncontrolled copies. Use compressed, columnar, and open formats where appropriate, with deliberate retention tiers.

COMPUTE DELIBERATELY

Process only what changed

Prefer incremental work, partition pruning, right-sized compute, workload isolation, and schedules tied to real SLAs.

DESIGN FOR CHANGE

Decouple the responsibilities

Automate, observe, version, and isolate components so one tool can evolve without rebuilding the entire idea.

Data engineers connect operational reality to analysts, scientists, products, leaders, and AI systems. We are not moving bytes for technology’s sake; we are creating dependable flow between people who own systems and people who need answers.

Knowledge check · Retrieval practice

Can you reason about it
without the diagram?

Answer all five questions, then reveal the explanations. A wrong answer is useful evidence about what to revisit.

1. What makes a Bronze layer valuable?

Bronze is the auditable safety net: close to what arrived, lightly transformed, and sufficient to rebuild downstream layers.

2. An event arrives after its reporting window was first emitted. Which concepts matter?

Streaming correctness depends on when events occurred, when the system believes a window is complete, and what to do with late arrivals.

3. Why should a partitioned pipeline be idempotent?

Retries and backfills are normal. Idempotency makes them safe by preventing accidental duplicates or compounding changes.

4. A large Spark join has one task that runs far longer than all others. What is a likely cause?

Uneven key distribution can overload one shuffle partition. Inspect partition sizes and the query plan before changing compute.

5. Which is the best first architecture question?

Architecture is a response to requirements and constraints. Tool selection comes after the job to be done is understood.

04 · EXPLAIN
